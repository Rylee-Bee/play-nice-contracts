## Why CC BY-SA 4.0 for specifications

A specification travels best under a license that strongly supports:

- **copying** — anyone can include the contracts verbatim in their
  own implementation documentation
- **quoting** — anyone can reference contract IDs, names, and text
  without restriction
- **implementing** — an implementer can build a tool that satisfies
  these contracts without licensing their tool under any specific
  terms
- **translating** — anyone can translate the contracts into other
  languages
- **redistributing** — anyone can ship the contracts (original or
  adapted) as part of their project, with attribution

The `ShareAlike` term means adaptations of these contracts must be
released under a compatible license. That is the right shape for
specifications: it keeps the canonical text and any forks of it
shareable while explicitly *not* forcing the implementer's
unrelated application into any particular license.
