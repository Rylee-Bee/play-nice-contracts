# Acknowledgement 🐝

Play Nice is a set of contracts and tooling for cooperation across
different participants. Some projects find these useful. This page
is a friendly way to leave a little trail back when Play Nice helped
make your project better.

Acknowledgement is **voluntary**. It is not a license requirement,
not a contract, not an endorsement, and not certification.

## Why a voluntary trail

Play Nice is built on the principle that **interoperability should
not force sameness**. A trail back home is a fingerprint, not a
chain. If Play Nice helped your project — by shaping its design,
influencing a contract, or informing how participants collaborate —
we'd love to know. If it didn't help, we'd also love to know that.

## How to acknowledge

There are two voluntary states. Use the one that fits your project.
Neither requires permission, neither grants you endorsement, and
neither obligates you to anything.

### 🐝 INSPIRED BY PLAY NICE

Play Nice influenced your project.

- **No compatibility claim.** Your project was shaped by reading or
  discussing Play Nice; it does not claim to satisfy any specific
  contract.
- **No certification claim.** You have not been audited or verified
  by Rylee, the Play Nice project, or anyone else.
- **No endorsement claim.** Acknowledgement is not an endorsement
  in either direction.

Where to put it: your README, your docs site, your `ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS`
or `THANKS` file, your about page. Pick a place that's appropriate
to your project.

### 🐝 IMPLEMENTS PLAY NICE

Your project itself claims to implement one or more identifiable
Play Nice contracts.

- This is a **downstream project claim**, not certification by the
  Play Nice project.
- Encourage identifying, where useful: which Play Nice revision
  or version your implementation targets; which specific contracts
  you implement; any attestation or evidence you produce (tests,
  recorded runs, conformance suites).
- The Play Nice project does not currently run a verification
  program. Do not present an `IMPLEMENTS` acknowledgement as if it
  were certified, audited, or endorsed.

Where to put it: the same options as `INSPIRED BY`. Be specific
about which contracts you implement and on what evidence.

## What you do not owe Play Nice

You do not owe Play Nice:

- your application
- your unrelated source code
- your private source
- your world
- your data
- your customer data
- your proprietary machinery

We would simply like to know what you learned.

## Failure letters are welcome

If you implemented Play Nice and it made something worse, didn't
cover what you needed, didn't translate, or created unnecessary
work, we want to hear about that. Open an issue on the canonical
repository (`https://github.com/Rylee-Bee/play-nice-contracts`)
or find a contact channel linked from there.

Failure is information. The judge is also a participant.

## A note on endorsement

A Play Nice acknowledgement, in either form, does **not** mean:

- Rylee reviewed the project
- Rylee endorses it
- Play Nice endorses it
- it is officially affiliated
- it passed certification
- every component of the project follows Play Nice

Project-identity and trademark rules for "official" Play Nice live
in `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## Keep the house. Send a letter back.

If you improve Play Nice itself, please consider bringing what you
learned home. Useful letters include:

- contract edge cases you found
- failure modes
- translation problems
- interoperability lessons
- accessibility lessons
- provenance techniques
- recovery patterns
- implementation techniques
- conformance difficulties
- case studies
- documentation improvements

Open an issue or pull request on the canonical repository. We read
every one.
