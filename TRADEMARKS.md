# Project Identity

Play Nice is a personal project by Rylee Hulgan. This page describes
how the name is used and how forks should distinguish themselves.

## The official project

- Canonical source: `https://github.com/Rylee-Bee/play-nice-contracts`
- Maintainer of record: Rylee Hulgan (the author)
- Official communication channels: GitHub issues and pull requests
  on the canonical repository

Nothing else is "official Play Nice" — not a fork, not a re-packaged
distribution, not a hosted service, not a tutorial site. If you
encounter something claiming to be official but not hosted under
the canonical URL above, that claim is incorrect.

## You are welcome to fork

You may fork, modify, and redistribute Play Nice under its licenses
(see `LICENSE`, `contracts/LICENSE.md`, and `docs/LICENSE.md`). You
may implement Play Nice contracts in your own project under any
license you choose for *your* code.

When you do, please use a distinct name for substantially modified
versions of the *canonical contracts* or *tooling* and do not imply
endorsement by, or affiliation with, the official project. Examples
of acceptable fork naming:

- "Play-Nice-fork" or "Play-Nice-something" for a light modification
- A completely different name ("CooperativeContracts") for a
  substantial fork or rewrite

Examples of unacceptable naming:

- "Official Play Nice" or "Play Nice by <your-org>" for a
  non-canonical fork
- "Play-Nice-Verified" or "Play-Nice-Certified" when the
  verification or certification is not from the maintainer of
  record — the Play Nice project does not currently run such a
  program, and the names are reserved

## Trademarks

"Play Nice" is used to identify the official project. It is **not**
a registered trademark; this page is descriptive, not a legal
grant. No ® or ™ is asserted.

The 🐝 bee emoji is used as an informal symbol for "Inspired by
Play Nice" and "Implements Play Nice" acknowledgements. The symbol
is not a logo, not a trademark, and conveys no endorsement in
either direction.

Nothing in this document grants you permission to use the
"Rylee-Bee" or "Rylee Hulgan" names for endorsement. Please use
your own name and identity in your fork.

## Acknowledgement vs endorsement

If you want to acknowledge Play Nice's influence on your project,
see `docs/ACKNOWLEDGEMENT.md`. Acknowledgement is informational;
it does not turn your project into an official Play Nice project,
and it does not require or imply Rylee's endorsement.
